Process

Three steps. Zero physical callbacks to start.

Traditional casting runs on waiting rooms and paper sign-in sheets. ON runs the same process as a data pipeline — every step logged, versioned, and visible to both sides.

01

Create a verified profile

One canonical record per person — reel, stats, availability, and history — verified once and reused for every brief after that. No re-submitting the same headshot to twenty inboxes.

02

Get matched, not buried

Directors post a brief; the engine routes it against verified talent that actually fits — role, range, availability — instead of an open call flooding one inbox.

03

Confirm and shoot

Self-tapes, scheduling, and booking confirmation happen inside the platform. The first time anyone needs to be in a physical room is the shoot itself.

The engine

Built on the same pipeline discipline as our post-production work.

aboustate.tech runs media production and post on one strict pipeline — every asset versioned, every handoff logged, nothing lost between departments. ON is that same infrastructure pointed at a different asset type: people.

A submission moves through the system the way a shot moves through an edit bay — timestamped, routed, and never sitting in an inbox someone forgot to open.
